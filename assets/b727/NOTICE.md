# Boeing 727-230 model

Source: https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/727-230
Revision: ba983e8ef20ec363bd7d56c6fbc9aa24ea329375

Original modeling by Heiko Schulz and Emmanuel Baranger, with the FlightGear 727 project contributors including Peter Brendt and Israel Hernandez. Cockpit by it0uchpods and Legoboyvdlp. Additional credits are retained in the original files in the source archive.

License: GNU GPL version 2. See [COPYING.txt](COPYING.txt) and [corresponding source and conversion scripts](source.zip).

Adaptations: AC3D/XML conversion to glTF, compressed WebP textures, original Lufthansa paint, source-based flap/slat and landing gear animations, simplified live flight and engine instruments, pilot camera placement, and textured crash pieces. Flight dynamics and aircraft systems are simplified game models. Other cockpit controls remain decorative.
