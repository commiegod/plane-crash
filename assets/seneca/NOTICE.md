# Piper PA-34 Seneca II

Aircraft source: https://github.com/FGMEMBERS/SenecaII
Revision: dd6b839bc9c7f559c0c156db1c963546c2035da2

Aircraft by Torsten Dreyer and FlightGear community contributors. Shared analog instruments come from https://github.com/FGDATA/fgdata (the exact files used are preserved in the source archive). Original author notices remain in the archived files.

Aircraft license: GNU GPL version 2 or later, as declared in SenecaII-set.xml, SenecaII-base.xml and Models/SenecaII.xml. Shared instruments use the upstream GPL terms. [License](COPYING.txt); [corresponding source and conversion scripts](source.zip).

Adaptations: indexed glTF conversion, compressed WebP textures, original D-GEAR paint, counter-rotating independent propellers, retractable gear visibility, pilot camera, simplified live analog instruments and matching authored wreckage. Radio and auxiliary cockpit controls are decorative; this is a game, not a training simulation. The Seneca II replaces the procedural twin-engine trainer and has six total seats (five passengers and one pilot).
