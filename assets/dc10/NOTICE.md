# McDonnell Douglas DC-10-30 model

Source: https://github.com/Octal450/DC-10
Revision: bb0ddb80be44b1435a4307fa62873d472469eab1

FlightGear DC-10 community aircraft, including work by Josh Davidson (Octal450), Gary R. Neely, and the contributors credited in the original source files.

License: GNU GPL version 2; [license](COPYING.txt), [corresponding source and conversion scripts](source.zip).

Adapted for Flight Simulator: AC3D/XML converted to indexed glTF, WebP textures, GE -30 instrument fit selected, original American/Air France/Lufthansa paint, live airspeed/altitude/vertical-speed/bank needles and attitude texture movement, pilot camera placement, gear visibility and textured crash sections. A small depth offset keeps the attitude texture visible through the converted panel. Other cockpit switches and displays are decorative. The game is a simplified simulator.
