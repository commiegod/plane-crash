Modifications for Flight Simulator: AC3D/XML conversion to glTF, indexed geometry, WebP textures, source material transparency correction, selected original airline liveries, live simplified flight displays, selectable gear visibility, and textured crash mesh splitting. Aircraft geometry and cockpit artwork remain derived from the original community model. Switches are decorative; this is not a complete aircraft systems simulator.
