# Douglas DC-3 model

Source: https://github.com/tdammers/Douglas-Dc3
Revision: d45b0f7bfaedc1fed098ed8b841792f0c339e854

FlightGear DC-3 by Emmanuel Baranger, the PAF team and community contributors credited in the included original source files and contributors.txt.

License: GNU GPL version 2. See [COPYING.txt](COPYING.txt) and [corresponding source and conversion scripts](source.zip).

Adaptations: landplane with traditional analog panel, AC3D/XML to indexed glTF, WebP textures, original Air France paint, independently driven propellers, simplified main-gear retraction, tailwheel ground stance, pilot camera and textured wreckage. Airspeed, altitude, vertical speed, attitude, heading and engine RPM gauges use simplified game values. Other controls and systems remain decorative; this is not a full FlightGear systems simulation.
