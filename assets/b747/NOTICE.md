# Boeing 747-400 model

Source: https://github.com/gijsrooy/747-400
Revision: 86cfa0a643489f63e1fac29ca3efcc149fea5ae7

FlightGear community aircraft led by Gijs de Rooy, with contributors credited in the included 747-400-set.xml and original source files.

License: GNU GPL version 2. See [COPYING.txt](COPYING.txt) and [corresponding source and conversion scripts](source.zip).

Adaptations: AC3D/XML to indexed glTF, compressed WebP textures, original KLM paint, working simplified primary flight/navigation/engine displays, selectable gear visibility, pilot camera placement and textured wreckage. Other cockpit switches and standby instruments remain decorative; aircraft systems and crash physics are simplified game models.
