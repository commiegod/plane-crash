# Emergency landing recovery

Intact belly/gear-collapse slides retain limited left/right steering and braking. Authority falls with speed and failed controls; collapsed gear has weak braking. Broken-up wreckage remains uncontrolled. Grounded, still-flyable aircraft retain their existing taxi and takeoff behavior.

The report button is available immediately without opening automatically. Moving incidents are marked IN PROGRESS, update if left open, and become FINAL when cabin-bearing assemblies rest. There is no evacuation countdown. Later ground impacts can increase injuries/fatalities, and intact slides can strike buildings and transition to greater breakup. Damage and fatalities never decrease during a single incident. A gentle later contact cannot restore casualties or exits. Intact airframes that fall into a severe secondary touchdown are rebuilt from the new impact.

Damaged but successful landings acknowledge wing/system damage. Extended gear remains in the authored wreck and participates in existing deformation instead of vanishing when collapsed. Geometry and control response remain approximate gameplay models.

Validation: all 11 aircraft tested for skid steering/braking, provisional reports, pause, settling, gentle-impact survival, and landing with partial wing damage plus an engine out. Secondary building collision, casualty conservation, monotonic fatalities, existing landing/crash/scrape regressions. Browser checks verified optional provisional report and retained collapsed gear. Physical iPad testing not performed.
