# Landing contact — October 21, 2026

Touchdowns now use descent, attitude, lateral motion, gear state, obstacles and a generous overspeed boundary. Runway heading and a minimum airspeed no longer turn gentle contact into a crash. Smooth versus hard landings receive distinct messages and reports while retaining taxi and repeat-takeoff controls. Existing gear-collapse, belly-slide and severe breakup paths remain available.

Sliding metal emits sparks on pavement, dust on grass. Non-burning slides no longer leave a burn mark.

Validation: landing-contact tests across all eleven aircraft; repeat-flight tests; B727 handling at 30/60/120 Hz; contact-safety and crash-settling suites. Browser checked hard-landing controls and detailed B727 belly-slide presentation. Physical iPad testing remains outstanding. Thresholds remain gameplay approximations.
