# Piper Seneca II update

The last procedural aircraft, the twin-engine trainer, now uses the free FlightGear Piper PA-34 Seneca II model with original D-GEAR paint and a modeled analog cockpit. All ten aircraft selections now use authored 3D assets.

Two counter-rotating propellers respond independently to engine failures. Flight and RPM needles follow simplified game state. Gear retracts, and intact, sectioned and fragmented wrecks retain the same aircraft geometry and textures. Six total seats: five passengers and one pilot. Radio and auxiliary cockpit systems remain decorative.

Download: 1.80 MB model plus 0.61 MB textures, loaded on demand. GPL source, conversion scripts and attribution are included.

Validation: gameplay regression suite; all ten aircraft land-stop-restart-takeoff-land-crash sequences; stall recovery; authored wreck preservation and deformation. Browser checks cover exterior, cockpit, independent propeller response, counter-rotation, gear visibility and sectioned wreckage. Grass-touchdown fixtures now use each aircraft's approach speed rather than an airliner speed for the smaller Seneca. Physical iPad testing remains outstanding.
