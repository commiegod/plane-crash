# DC-9-32 aircraft update

Replaces the procedural DC-9 with the free community DC-9-32 exterior and flight deck. Delta is the supported source paint. Both pilots' airspeed, altitude and attitude instruments respond to flight state; the compass card, vertical-speed needle and independent engine N1/N2 indications are connected. Gear follows the existing gear control, and crashes retain the authored model and paint.

The optimized model is 1.72 MB with approximately 0.3 MB of textures. License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; see assets/dc9/NOTICE.md. This asset is restricted to noncommercial use with attribution and share-alike terms. The auxiliary cockpit systems remain decorative and the flight/engine indications are simplified game values.

Validation: full gameplay suite, all ten aircraft's repeated landing/takeoff/crash sequences, authored wreck preservation, asset references and license archive. Browser checks covered cockpit/exterior, both speed gauges, independent engine needles, altitude drum, gear and all three wreck configurations. Physical iPad testing remains outstanding.

Remaining procedural aircraft: private jet, C-130 cargo plane and twin trainer.
