# DC-3 authored aircraft update

The DC-3 now uses the free FlightGear community exterior, cabin and traditional cockpit. Air France is the supported source paint. Source, conversion scripts and GPLv2 terms are provided under assets/dc3.

- Independent propellers and engine RPM indications respond to each engine.
- Both pilots' airspeed and altitude gauges are animated, with attitude, vertical speed and heading bindings.
- Main gear retraction, fixed tailwheel and low-speed nose-up ground stance.
- Source geometry and paint retained for intact, three-section and fragmented wreckage.
- Indexing and WebP compression reduce the model from 72.5 MB to 21.7 MB; textures total about 2 MB. Models load on selection.

Checked: browser cockpit/exterior, propeller independence, duplicated gauge bindings, gear, three wreck configurations, source archive and texture references; gameplay regressions, all-ten-aircraft repeated landing/takeoff sequences, stalls, and authored wreck/deformation tests. No errors in inspected browser views. Physical iPad Safari validation remains outstanding.

Flight indications and damage remain simplified game systems. Ancillary cockpit switches and systems are decorative. DC-9, private jet, cargo plane and twin trainer still use procedural geometry.
