# C-130 Hercules update

The cargo aircraft now uses the free FlightGear C-130 exterior and classic analog cockpit. Its four-blade propellers match the source model, so the visible name is C-130 Hercules rather than C-130J. Original French-marked paint replaces airline color choices for this aircraft.

Four propellers respond independently to engine failure. Airspeed, altitude, attitude, heading, vertical speed and four RPM needles follow game state. Gear follows the gear control. Intact, sectioned and fragmented crashes retain the authored model. Auxiliary cockpit systems are decorative and gauge values are simplified.

Model size: 2.66 MB plus 0.27 MB of textures, loaded on demand. GPLv2 source, credits and conversion scripts are included.

Validation: full gameplay suite; ten-aircraft land-stop-restart-takeoff-land-crash sequences; stall/recovery; authored wreck preservation and deformation. Browser checks verify exterior/cockpit, independent engine failure and propeller/gauge response, gear visibility and sectioned crash geometry. Physical iPad testing remains outstanding.

Remaining procedural aircraft: twin-engine trainer.
